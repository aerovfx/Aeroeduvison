METHOD 1: Via Network Tab Icon (FASTEST)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1:  Press F12 to open DevTools

Step 2:  Click "Network" tab (at top of DevTools)

Step 3:  Look for this icon in toolbar: 🚫
         (It might be a circle with a line through it)
         Text near it might say "Request blocking"

Step 4:  Click the 🚫 icon
         → A small panel appears

Step 5:  You'll see a checkbox:
         ☐ Enable request blocking
         
         UNCHECK IT! (make box empty)

Step 6:  Close the panel

Step 7:  Press: Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows)
         (Hard refresh)
